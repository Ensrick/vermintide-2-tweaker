# Dynamic Cosmetic Portraits — Development Notes

Technical reference for adding new portraits and understanding how the swap
works. Read alongside `CHANGELOG.md` (history) and `TODO.md` (open work).

---

## ⚠️ STOP — read this before generating ANY portrait asset

**Use the script. Do not free-hand resize or compose alpha.**

```powershell
.\tools\add_portrait.ps1 -SourcePng "<path to 110x130 source>" -HatKey "<character_key>"
```

The script encodes the only correct workflow. Free-handing it has cost
multiple sessions across both `cosmetics_tweaker v0.7.x` and
`dynamic_cosmetic_portraits v0.1.0` / `v0.1.1` / `v0.1.2`, all of which
shipped broken portraits because the AI agent guessed at:

1. **Resize behavior** — bicubic-resizing a 110×130 source to 86×108 / 60×70
   does NOT produce correct HUD/small output by itself. Without alpha
   masking, RGB pixels at the canvas corners poke past the in-game frame's
   hex cutout and visibly overflow the frame in-game.
2. **"Just copy 110×130 to all three filenames"** — also wrong. The HUD and
   matchmaking widgets render the texture at their native widget size; the
   engine doesn't add an alpha mask for you. Same overflow.
3. **Alpha-mask shape** — the hex/shield silhouette at HUD (86×108) and
   small (60×70) is identical for every portrait at a given size. It's
   defined by the in-game frame widget's cutout, not by portrait content.
   The script borrows the alpha channel from `mercenary_hat_0001` (Estalia)
   automatically.

The three correct outputs per portrait, generated by the script:

| Variant | Filename | Size | Alpha |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero-select | `medium_portrait_kruber_<key>.png` | 110×130 | None — full canvas opaque |
| HUD | `portrait_kruber_<key>.png` | 86×108 | Hex-shaped (corners A=0) |
| Matchmaking | `small_portrait_kruber_<key>.png` | 60×70 | Hex-shaped (corners A=0) |

The script also generates the matching `.texture` and `.material` files,
verifies alpha at corners, and prints the next manual wiring steps
(`_hat_portrait_map`, `_data.lua`, `.package`, version bump, build).

**If you must change the script** — first read this entire DEVELOPMENT.md
top-to-bottom, then test the result in-game on at least:
- The HUD player frame (Kruber Mercenary equipped with the new hat)
- The hero-select screen
- The matchmaking lobby

## What this mod does

Swaps `SPProfiles[<profile>].careers[<career>].portrait_image` and
`.picking_image` at runtime based on the equipped hat or outfit. Every VT2 UI
surface that displays a character portrait derives its texture name from these
two fields, so a single source-level swap propagates everywhere with no
per-widget hooks.

## Architecture

### One swap, every surface

VT2 builds portrait texture names from `career_settings.portrait_image` at
display time:

| Context | Naming | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| HUD unit frame | `<base>` directly | 86×108 |
| Hero selection | `medium_<base>` | 110×130 |
| ESC menu / tab overlay | `<base>` or `medium_<base>` | varies |
| Matchmaking / rewards | `small_<base>` | 60×70 |
| End-of-round | `<base>` | 86×108 |

Mutating `portrait_image` once flips all of them.

### SPProfiles structure

```lua
SPProfiles[5]                              -- Kruber (empire_soldier)
SPProfiles[5].careers[1]                   -- Mercenary (es_mercenary)
  .portrait_image = "unit_frame_portrait_kruber_mercenary"
  .picking_image  = "medium_unit_frame_portrait_kruber_mercenary"
```

Plain Lua tables — fully mutable. The mod saves the originals once, then
swaps to a custom `portrait_kruber_mercenary_<key>` and
`medium_portrait_kruber_mercenary_<key>` pair when a tracked hat/outfit is
equipped, and restores the originals when no portrait matches or the mod
unloads. DCP has no settings page; swapping is always on.

### Skin-over-hat priority

Outfits (slot_skin) replace the head model regardless of which hat is
equipped, so the `_skin_portrait_map` lookup runs first; the
`_hat_portrait_map` is the fallback. See `_sync_portrait_settings()` in
`scripts/mods/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits.lua`.

### Where `_sync_portrait_settings()` is called

- `mod.on_game_state_changed` — keep load, mission load, menu transitions.
- `UnitFrameUI.draw` (`hook_safe`) — catches the first frame after materials
  are ready. Becomes a cheap no-op once the swap is active.
- `mod.on_unload` calls `_restore_portrait_settings()` on cleanup.

### Material readiness probe

`_check_portrait_materials_ready()` walks every UIRenderer it can find
(`Managers.ui`, `Managers.matchmaking`, `Managers.transition`, `_ingame_ui`,
`_hud`, `unit_frames_handler`). It first proves representative HUD/small
identifiers resolve through the private atlas at their exact dimensions, then
probes `Gui.material()` for the shared atlas and representative standalone
medium material. Once one GUI satisfies the complete contract, the flag flips
and stays flipped for the session; otherwise DCP leaves the vanilla portrait
active.

This replaced the v0.7.52 approach of hooking `UIRenderer.create` — VMF
destroys+recreates the renderer in its own hook, so the user-mod hook never
fires. Probe directly.

## Adding a new portrait — full workflow

This is the workflow followed for every portrait shipped so far. Treat it as
the contract.

### 1 — Author the source PNG

Required source format:
- **110×130 PNG** (matches the hero-select widget native size)
- The face/headgear should fill the canvas
- No alpha needed in the source — alpha masking is added per-size below

Source PSDs in `D:\Game Mods\Vermintide 2 modding\`:
- `Kruber Portrait.psd` — older template (Estalia / Hellequin / Felix /
  VT1 etc.)
- `Kruber Framed Medium.psd` — newer template (Plumed Horseshoe)

Export naming: `Kruber Portrait (<Cosmetic Name>).png` or
`Kruber Framed (<Cosmetic Name>).png`. The filename only matters for human
bookkeeping; the in-mod filename is what the engine sees.

### 2 — Identify the cosmetic key

Look up the in-game key via `CHARACTER_COSMETIC_CATALOG.md`. Hat keys look
like `mercenary_hat_0003`; outfit keys look like `skin_es_mercenary_1003`
or `skin_es_default`. The catalog is the only reliable mapping from a
player-facing name (e.g. "Plumed Horseshoe") to the opaque key.

### 3 — Generate three sized variants with proper alpha

The in-game UI uses three texture variants, each at its native widget
size with appropriate alpha:

| Variant | Filename | Size | Alpha |
|---|---|---|---|
| HUD | `portrait_kruber_<key>.png` | 86×108 | Hex/shield-shaped mask (corners A=0) |
| Hero-select | `medium_portrait_kruber_<key>.png` | 110×130 | None (fully opaque) |
| Matchmaking | `small_portrait_kruber_<key>.png` | 60×70 | Hex/shield-shaped mask (corners A=0) |

The HUD and small variants need **shaped alpha** that defines the
visible silhouette inside the in-game frame widget. Without it,
content overflows past the frame's hex cutout.

The alpha mask shape is **identical across all portraits** at a given
size — it's defined by the in-game frame widget's window cutout, not
by the portrait content. So we just borrow the alpha channel from any
existing working portrait (e.g. `mercenary_hat_0001`).

```powershell
Add-Type -AssemblyName System.Drawing
$src    = "D:\Game Mods\Vermintide 2 modding\<source>.png"
$dstDir = "C:\Users\danjo\source\repos\vermintide-2-tweaker\dynamic_cosmetic_portraits\gui\1080p\single_textures\custom_portraits"
$key    = "kruber_<key>"   # e.g. "kruber_mercenary_hat_0003"

# 1. Medium: copy source verbatim (110×130, no alpha mask)
Copy-Item $src (Join-Path $dstDir "medium_portrait_$key.png") -Force

# 2 & 3. HUD and small: bicubic resize + alpha from reference portrait
$sourceBmp = New-Object System.Drawing.Bitmap $src
foreach ($v in @(@{prefix="";       w=86; h=108; ref="portrait_kruber_mercenary_hat_0001.png"},
                  @{prefix="small_"; w=60; h=70;  ref="small_portrait_kruber_mercenary_hat_0001.png"})) {
    $resized = New-Object System.Drawing.Bitmap $v.w, $v.h
    $g = [System.Drawing.Graphics]::FromImage($resized)
    $g.InterpolationMode = [System.Drawing.Drawing2D.InterpolationMode]::HighQualityBicubic
    $g.SmoothingMode     = [System.Drawing.Drawing2D.SmoothingMode]::HighQuality
    $g.PixelOffsetMode   = [System.Drawing.Drawing2D.PixelOffsetMode]::HighQuality
    $g.DrawImage($sourceBmp, 0, 0, $v.w, $v.h)
    $g.Dispose()

    $refBmp = New-Object System.Drawing.Bitmap (Join-Path $dstDir $v.ref)
    $output = New-Object System.Drawing.Bitmap $v.w, $v.h, ([System.Drawing.Imaging.PixelFormat]::Format32bppArgb)
    for ($y = 0; $y -lt $v.h; $y++) {
        for ($x = 0; $x -lt $v.w; $x++) {
            $rgb = $resized.GetPixel($x, $y)
            $alpha = $refBmp.GetPixel($x, $y).A
            $output.SetPixel($x, $y, [System.Drawing.Color]::FromArgb($alpha, $rgb.R, $rgb.G, $rgb.B))
        }
    }
    $output.Save((Join-Path $dstDir "$($v.prefix)portrait_$key.png"), [System.Drawing.Imaging.ImageFormat]::Png)
    $resized.Dispose(); $refBmp.Dispose(); $output.Dispose()
}
$sourceBmp.Dispose()
```

### Why this works

The in-game HUD and matchmaking surfaces draw a frame widget on top of
the portrait at a fixed position. The frame does not perform clipping, so
the PNG needs shaped alpha. In addition, the alpha-sensitive HUD/small
identifiers must resolve through `UIAtlasHelper`: vanilla then draws them
with `Gui.bitmap_uv`. Issue #526 proved that the former standalone
`Gui.bitmap` branch exposed the rectangular quad even when the committed
PNG alpha was byte-for-byte identical to the canonical mask.

Without the alpha mask, the resized RGB content fills the entire
86×108 / 60×70 rectangle. The frame's hex cutout is smaller than the
rectangle, so RGB pixels at the canvas corners aren't covered by the
frame artwork — they show as visible "overflow" past the frame edge.
This was the v0.1.1 / v0.1.2 Plumed Horseshoe bug.

### 4 — Generate the medium metadata and rebuild the shared atlas

Only the 110x130 medium PNG gets standalone `.texture` and `.material`
metadata. HUD and small PNGs are source sprites for the shared private atlas:

```powershell
.\tools\rebuild_portrait_atlas.ps1
```

That deterministic script rewrites:

- `textures/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits/dcp_portrait_atlas.png`
- `materials/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits/dcp_portrait_atlas.lua`

The atlas-wide `.texture` and `.material` definitions are stable, checked-in
files beside those generated outputs. The medium templates are:

`<prefix>portrait_<key>.texture`:
```
common = {
    input = {
        filename = "gui/1080p/single_textures/custom_portraits/<prefix>portrait_<key>"
    }
    output = {
        apply_processing = true
        category = ""
        cut_alpha_threshold = 0.5
        enable_cut_alpha_threshold = false
        format = "DXT5"
        mipmap_filter = "kaiser"
        mipmap_filter_wrap_mode = "mirror"
        mipmap_keep_original = false
        mipmap_num_largest_steps_to_discard = 0
        mipmap_num_smallest_steps_to_discard = 0
        srgb = true
        streamable = false
    }
}
```

`<prefix>portrait_<key>.material`:
```
<prefix>portrait_<key> = {
    material_contexts = {
        surface_material = ""
    }

    shader = "gui:DIFFUSE_MAP"

    textures = {
        diffuse_map = "gui/1080p/single_textures/custom_portraits/<prefix>portrait_<key>"
    }

    variables = {
    }
}
```

The block name inside the `.material` file MUST match the filename
(without extension) — Stingray names Gui materials after the file, not after
the block name, but mismatched names break the loader silently.

`tools/add_portrait.ps1` writes the medium templates and invokes the atlas
rebuild automatically. Do not author HUD/small standalone metadata: VMF does
not allow one identifier to be both a standalone texture and an atlas sprite.

### 5 — Wire up the lua

In `scripts/mods/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits.lua`:

- Add the medium path to `_PORTRAIT_STANDALONE_MATERIALS`. The HUD/small
  identifier list is derived from the generated atlas descriptor at runtime.
- Add an entry to `_hat_portrait_map` (for hats) or `_skin_portrait_map`
  (for outfits) keyed by the cosmetic key:

```lua
mercenary_hat_0003 = {  -- Plumed Horseshoe
    hud    = "portrait_kruber_mercenary_hat_0003",
    medium = "medium_portrait_kruber_mercenary_hat_0003",
    small  = "small_portrait_kruber_mercenary_hat_0003",
},
```

### 6 — Wire up the data file

In `scripts/mods/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits_data.lua`:

- Add only the medium identifier to `_texture_names`. HUD/small names are
  discovered by VMF from the generated atlas descriptor and must not appear in the
  standalone list.
- The existing atlas registration and `_build_injections()` add one shared
  atlas material plus all medium materials to each renderer creator. Keep the
  injection entries nested; flat strings are silently dropped by VMF.

### 7 — Wire up the package

In `resource_packages/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits/dynamic_cosmetic_portraits.package`:

- Add only the new medium material and texture paths. The shared atlas package
  rows are already present and the rebuild script updates their contents.

### 8 — Bump version, build, deploy

```powershell

$exe = "C:\Users\danjo\source\repos\vermintide-2-tweaker\tools\vmb-launcher\bin\Release\net9.0-windows\win-x64\publish\VMBLauncher.exe"
```

If a hash mismatch is reported, do **not** consider the deploy complete.
(The legacy `deploy_all.ps1` shim that previously covered this flow was
removed 2026-05-21 — use `VMBLauncher.exe deploy <mod>` or `tools\ship\ship.ps1` directly.)

### 9 — Test in-game

1. Launch VT2.
2. Equip the hat/outfit on Kruber Mercenary.
3. Verify HUD portrait, hero-select medium, and matchmaking small all swap.
4. Run `portrait_diag` if anything's wrong: it dumps career_settings state,
   material readiness on every GUI handle, and the current detected hat/skin
   key.

## VMF inject_materials — renderer-creator keys

VMF's `inject_materials(mod, ui_renderer_creator, ...)`
(custom_textures.lua:191) reads `ui_renderer_creator` from
`debug.traceback()` at frame 4 and matches it against the **basename of
the `.lua` file**.

If a custom material is registered with creator `"X"` only, ANY view
whose creator-detection lands on a different file will crash with
`Material 'X' not found in Gui` at `ui_passes.lua:134` the moment it
tries to draw a widget that references X.

### CRITICAL: Lua 5.1 tail-call elimination determines what frame 4 is

Many VT2 UI views chain through:

```
caller.lua  →  IngameUI:create_ui_renderer  →  view_settings.ui_renderer_function  →  UIRenderer.create
```

Both intermediate functions use `return f(...)` which is a **tail call**
in Lua 5.1 — those frames are stripped from the stack. So the `.lua`
file VMF sees at frame 4 is `caller.lua`, NOT `ingame_ui_settings.lua`
(the inner factory).

Implication: registering only the inner factory (`ingame_ui_settings`)
is NOT enough. **Every entry-point file that initiates the
renderer-creation chain must be listed.**

### Default minimum for a portrait/character mod (v0.1.6 baseline)

```lua
local _renderer_creators = {
    -- main HUD path (unit frames, tab overlay)
    "ingame_ui",
    -- inner factory (catches non-tail-called paths and direct calls)
    "ingame_ui_settings",
    -- HeroView entry points (Esc-menu sub-views)
    "hero_view",
    "hero_view_state_loot",            -- Spoils of War
    "hero_view_state_store",           -- Lohner's Emporium / cosmetics shop
    "hero_view_state_weave_forge",     -- Athanor / Weave forge
    -- StartGame entry points
    "start_game_state_settings_overview",
    "store_item_purchase_popup",
    "store_welcome_popup",
    -- end-of-mission
    "level_end_view_base",
    "level_end_view_versus",
    -- Chaos Wastes overworld map (uses portraits)
    "game_mode_map_deus",
    -- Managers.ui:create_ui_renderer chain
    "ui_manager",
}
```

### How to find the right creator name when a new crash hits

1. **Read the crash error:** `Material 'X' not found in Gui at ui_passes.lua:134`
   → some renderer didn't get our materials injected.
2. **Identify the UI surface** the user was on (pause-menu sub-view,
   end-of-mission screen, in-keep menu, etc.).
3. **Find renderer-creation call sites** in VT2 source:

   ```
   grep -rn "create_ui_renderer\|UIRenderer\.create\b" Vermintide-2-Source-Code/scripts | grep -v "draw\|destroy\|clear\|begin\|end\|create_video\|create_bitmap"
   ```

4. **Trace from the user-facing surface** back to the
   `:create_ui_renderer(world, ...)` call. The basename of THAT file
   (the entry point) is the creator name to add — not the inner
   factory, because tail-call elimination strips intermediate frames.
5. **Add it to `_renderer_creators`** in
   `dynamic_cosmetic_portraits_data.lua`, bump version, build, deploy,
   restart VT2.

### How crashes manifested in dynamic_cosmetic_portraits history

| Version | Surface that crashed | Missing creator |
|---|---|---|
| v0.1.4 | pause-menu buttons (HeroView, OptionsView, etc.) | `ingame_ui_settings` and/or `hero_view` |
| v0.1.5 | Spoils of War loot menu | `hero_view_state_loot` |
| v0.1.6 | (none reported as of 2026-05-08) | comprehensive list shipped |

### How to apply

When registering custom materials via VMF
`custom_gui_textures.ui_renderer_injections`:

1. **Always enumerate multiple creators** — at minimum the list above.
2. **`Gui.material(gui, name)` returns nil silently** for missing
   materials, so a `_gui_has_material` probe is the only reliable
   diagnostic. See `dynamic_cosmetic_portraits.lua` for the reference
   pattern.
3. **When a new crash hits**, follow the 5-step procedure above to find
   the missing creator. Don't guess at the inner factory — trace to the
   entry-point file.
4. **VMF doesn't error or warn** when a creator string never matches
   anything — silent failure. The only signal is the eventual
   `Material X not found in Gui` crash.

**Minimum creators for a portrait/character mod:** `ingame_ui` +
`ingame_ui_settings` + `level_end_view_base` + `level_end_view_versus`.
Missing `ingame_ui_settings` = pause-menu crash.

## Dead ends — do not retry

Captured from the cosmetics_tweaker pre-split history (v0.7.37–v0.7.62).
These were systematically tested and confirmed non-functional; reattempting
will burn a session.

1. **Per-widget content swap via `UnitFrameUI.draw` hook** — only catches
   the HUD unit frame. Misses hero selection (`content.portrait`), ESC menu,
   tab overlay, end-of-round.
2. **`Material.set_texture()` on Gui materials** — Gui pipeline is separate;
   the call no-ops.
3. **`Gui.create_material()` / `Gui.create()`** — both nil in this VT2 build.
4. **Manual `UIRenderer._injected_material_sets` append** — only affects
   future `UIRenderer.create` calls; existing renderers have their materials
   already baked in. VMF's `custom_gui_textures` API is the correct path.
5. **`UIRenderer.create` hook to detect material injection** — VMF
   destroys+recreates the renderer internally inside its own hook. The
   user-mod hook never fires. `Gui.material()` probe is the only reliable
   readiness check.
6. **Multi-definition `.material` files** — Stingray creates exactly one Gui
   material per file, named after the filename. Multiple `name = { … }`
   blocks inside one file are ignored.
7. **Hand-written UIAtlasHelper hooks** — VMF already owns the helper hooks.
   Register the private atlas through `custom_gui_textures.atlases`; a second
   mod hook would duplicate ownership. Conversely, do not return HUD/small
   portraits to standalone registration: #526 proves their `Gui.bitmap`
   fallback is not equivalent to vanilla's `Gui.bitmap_uv` atlas path.

## Authoring notes

### Alpha & sizing notes

The in-game UI uses three texture variants per portrait. Each variant
matches the corresponding widget's native size and has the appropriate
alpha mask:

| Variant | Size | Alpha |
|---|---|---|
| HUD | 86×108 | Shaped mask (corners A=0, hex/shield silhouette) |
| Hero-select | 110×130 | No mask (full opacity) |
| Matchmaking | 60×70 | Shaped mask (corners A=0, hex/shield silhouette) |

The alpha mask is the same shape for every portrait at a given size —
it's defined by the in-game frame widget's octagonal cutout, not by
per-portrait content. The `mercenary_hat_0001` (Estalia) HUD and small
files are good reference sources to copy alpha from when authoring new
portraits.

Medium standalone portraits and the private atlas's normal material use
`gui:DIFFUSE_MAP`. The attempted `gui_gradient:DIFFUSE_MAP:MASKED` standalone
correction for #526 compiled and passed `Gui.material` readiness, but rendered
the HUD portrait fully transparent. Do not infer visible output from material
lookup alone. HUD/small portraits now retain canonical alpha inside the atlas
and follow vanilla's `Gui.bitmap_uv` renderer path.

**(#526) The HUD mask must conform to the VANILLA silhouette, not a
content-derived cutout.** Pre-0.1.20-dev the hud-size mask was derived
from portrait content: it was wider than the vanilla window (446 opaque
pixels outside it, including canvas-top contact), so portraits visibly
bled outside the octagonal frame on the mission-completion score screen
(and, subtly, on the HUD/Tab list — same texture, same 86×108 draw rect).
The canonical silhouette now ships as
`tools/vanilla_hud_alpha_mask_86x108.png` (median alpha of 19 vanilla
84×108 hud portraits extracted from the bundles, scaled to the 86×108
draw rect), and `add_portrait.ps1` throws if a generated HUD png has any
opaque pixel outside it. Note the frame ring drawn on top is a thin
octagonal OUTLINE with a transparent exterior — it does not cover
overflow the way the hero-select frame does at medium size. The mask gate is a
necessary authoring invariant, while the atlas contract is the renderer
invariant; both are covered by offline and runtime checks.

Hero selection renders the medium 110×130 file at native size, with the
frame widget drawn on top. The frame artwork is opaque around its hex
cutout, so it covers any content past the cutout — shaped alpha isn't
needed at the medium size.

HUD and matchmaking render the smaller variants at their native widget
size. The frame widget on top is opaque around the hex cutout, but the
shaped alpha on the portrait texture is the cleaner mask — without it,
RGB pixels at the canvas corners poke past the frame's cutout edge.

### Hot-reload is unsafe

`Ctrl+Shift+R` will leave dangling C++ resource locks on loaded materials
and textures. There is no Lua-side fix. Restart VT2 after every redeploy.
This is documented in `feedback_hot_reload_unfixable.md` (Anthropic memory)
and applies to every mod with non-Lua resources.

### Forward references will crash

Lua locals are not hoisted. The `_check_portrait_materials_ready` ↔
`_collect_all_guis` order has been broken before and crashed the mod (v0.7.54).
When adding a function, search for callers above it before saving.

## Generalising beyond Kruber Mercenary

`_get_kruber_merc_hat_key()` and `_get_kruber_merc_skin_key()` are
hardcoded to `es_mercenary` and `SPProfiles[5].careers[1]`. Extending to
other careers needs:

1. Generic helpers that accept a career_name + profile/career indices.
2. A registry mapping `career_name` → `(profile_idx, career_idx)`.
3. Per-career portrait maps (or a single map keyed by career_name + cosmetic
   key).
4. Photoshop work for the new portraits (one set of three sizes per
   character × hat/outfit).

The data plumbing is straightforward. The asset authoring is the
load-bearing part — every shipped portrait represents a Photoshop session.
