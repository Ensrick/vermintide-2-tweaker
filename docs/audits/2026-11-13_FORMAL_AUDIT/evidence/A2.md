## 2. Architecture as built

### 2.1 cwv

**Entry and load order.** `character_weapon_variants.mod` declares one package root; `character_weapon_variants_data.lua:2` already `mod:dofile`s the dev animation picker to build its widget tree. The entry `character_weapon_variants.lua` is an ordered script of 97 `mod:dofile` calls (132 across the mod). Order is load-bearing and says so repeatedly ("call order is load-bearing", `character_weapon_variants.lua:479`; "It loads HERE, at the exact point the inline block ran", `:656`, `:819`, `:875`). Rough phases:

1. `:5-113` mostly policy modules loaded into one table `_om`; three already install side effects (smoke-bomb probe `:9`, re-wield coalescer `:112`, exact-pair channel `:113`).
2. `:166-202` peer-parity beacon (`cwv_peer_parity_present`).
3. `:222-255` `/cwv_regression_test` runner.
4. `:392-533` variant catalog, mace/hammer identity, Crowbill runtime, cross-access, core weapon-template constructors, sword/mace/tuskgor families.
5. `:556-649` Kruber crossbow patch of a VANILLA template + `MenuWorldPreviewer.equip_item` hook.
6. `:661-696` musket runtime and equip surface (phase 1).
7. `:718-810` first-person state-machine package leases, loaded synchronously at mod load.
8. `:824-907` husk residency, custom-mesh runtime (global `Unit.*`/`PackageManager.*` hooks), javelin, rapier.
9. `:916-982` bootstrap (Localize hook, vanilla unlocks), skin registry, illusion families, item registration owner.
10. `:1004-1295` transforms, Combat Styles install, legacy CIM save migration.
11. `:1345-1532` husk path, `BackendUtils.get_item_units` hook, identity transport (wire-safety hooks), world equipment, menu previews, commands and lifecycle callbacks.
12. `:1537-1574` in-game regression modules (four installed here plus one owner module they load).

**State ownership.** There is one shared mutable table, `_om`, created at `character_weapon_variants.lua:55` and passed by reference to almost every module. Measured: 286 distinct `_om.<field>` names, touched from 29 files, 22 fields assigned from two or more files (`scripts\A2_om.py`). Modules talk to each other by publishing functions onto `_om` and reading them late (`_om._cwv_key_for_item` is read from 14 files). The reason is stated in the code: the entry chunk hit Lua 5.1's limit of 200 local variables per function (`:17-18`, `:49-55`, `:397`), so state moved onto a table instead of into modules with interfaces. The "decomposition" of the former 8,800-line entry (`CODE_REVIEW.md:13`) was a verbatim cut ("Extracted verbatim from the entry file; behavior is unchanged", `_cwv_item_registration_owner.lua:23`).

**Hook surface.** 101 hooks on 101 distinct targets (81 `mod:hook`, 19 `mod:hook_safe`, 1 via an alias). Notable: four global engine C-API functions (`Unit.node`, `Unit.has_node`, `Unit.flow_event`, `Unit.set_flow_variable`, `_cwv_custom_mesh_runtime.lua:472-509`), `PackageManager.load/unload/has_loaded` (`:175-186`), `_G.Localize` (`_cwv_variant_bootstrap_owner.lua:194`), per-frame `CharacterStateHelper.move_on_ground/move_in_air` (`_cwv_mace_sword_runtime.lua:116-122`), `GenericAmmoUserExtension.update` (`_cwv_musket_ammo_pool.lua:299`), `WeaponSpreadExtension.update` (`_cwv_musket_equip_surface.lua:418`). `mod.update` is re-wrapped in a chain from three places (`_lib_peer_parity.lua:654`, once per parity instance; `_cwv_rewield_coalescer.lua:256`; `_cwv_item_identity_transport_owner.lua:556`). `mod.on_all_mods_loaded` is assigned three times; two chain the previous value, the first does not (`_cwv_musket_equip_surface.lua:304`), which is safe only because it happens to load first.

**RPC channels (VMF mod-to-mod, 8 in total).** `cwv_peer_parity_present` (entry `:172`), `cwv_damage_profiles_exact_v1` and `cwv_thrown_resources_exact_v1` (`_cwv_exact_wire_runtime.lua:152`, `:99`), `cwv_item_identity` (`_cwv_item_identity_transport_owner.lua:390`), `cwv_exact_pair_state_v1` (`_cwv_exact_pair_state.lua:12`), `cwv_combat_style_v1` (`_cwv_combat_styles.lua:21`), `cwv_crowbill_mode_v1` (`_cwv_crowbill_hammer_mode.lua:19`), `cwv_old_musket_mode_v1` (`_cwv_old_musket_wire.lua:322`).

**Vanilla tables mutated.** `ItemMasterList` (new rows, plus `can_wield` appends on four VANILLA items: `_cwv_cross_access.lua:18-45`, `_cwv_variant_bootstrap_owner.lua:228-263`), `Weapons` (new templates, plus `wield_anim_career_3p` written onto six VANILLA templates: entry `:556-571`, `_cwv_cross_access.lua:63-150`), `WeaponSkins.skins` / `skin_combinations` / `_matching_weapon_skin_item_keys` (`_cwv_item_registration_owner.lua:781-802`), `DamageProfileTemplates`, `PowerLevelTemplates`, `Projectiles`, `ProjectileUnitsFromUnitName` (`_cwv_exact_wire_runtime.lua:48`), `CareerSettings` slot lists (`_cwv_musket_equip_surface.lua` around `:223-304`), `DeusWeapons` / `DeusStartingWeaponTypeMapping`, and eight `NetworkLookup` tables: `item_names`, `weapon_skins`, `damage_profiles`, `pickup_names`, `husks`, `projectile_units`, `item_template_names`, `inventory_packages` (forward-only aliases, `_cwv_custom_mesh_runtime.lua:199-221`). None of these is restored on disable or unload (`_cwv_commands_lifecycle.lua:593-613`).

**Persistence.** One `mod:set` (per-instance Combat Style map, `_cwv_combat_styles.lua:1757`). cwv also writes into ANOTHER mod's saved data: `cim:set("forged_weapons", saved)` / `cim._cim_persist_crafts()` in the legacy style migration (entry `:1215-1294`).

**Cross-mod dependencies.** Hard, undeclared: `MoreItemsLibrary` (`_cwv_item_registration_owner.lua:674`; without it no backend item exists) and Crafting in Modded (`cim_dev`/`cim`) for acquiring anything above the 5-power seed (`/cwv_give` only prints "Craft ... through Crafting in Modded", `_cwv_variant_bootstrap_owner.lua:454`). Soft: `wt`/`wt_dev` private runtime state read by eight "envelope" modules (see A2-F04), `cosmetics_tweaker` (`_cwv_identity_descriptor_planner.lua:26,213`). Private members of other mods used: `cim._cim_get_craft`, `cim._cim_persist_crafts`. Exports consumed by siblings: `mod._cwv_resolve_item_key`, `mod._cwv_inventory_icons`, `mod._cwv_peer_appearance`, `mod._cwv_get_blacksmith_seed_identity_provider`, the `item_data.cwv_variant` marker.

**Assessment.** cwv is accreted patches wearing the costume of an architecture. The good part is real: the wire-safety model that emerged ("a mod value never rides a vanilla wire; the vanilla wire carries the base item; the real identity travels as string keys on a mod channel"; `_cwv_cosmetic_skin_wire.lua:52-58`, `_cwv_item_identity_transport_owner.lua:93-103`) is the right design and is applied carefully to loadout, skins, damage profiles and the javelin. But it was reached one crash at a time, each fix is its own module with its own vocabulary, and one family (the Outrider launcher) was later patched in the opposite direction (A2-F01). The catalog is data-driven only for the trivial case (swap a mesh on a base weapon). Everything interesting, which is most variants, is bespoke code: about 5,600 lines for the two muskets, 3,000 for the javelin, 6,600 for Combat Styles. The module split is by "what was extracted when", not by interface; the shared `_om` table means any module can break any other and the load order is the contract. One fifth of all lines are comments, mostly incident history, and the documents describe a file that no longer exists (`ENGINE_SURFACE.md` cites 28 line numbers in the 4,000-11,000 range of a 1,574-line file).

### 2.2 WOC

**Entry and load order.** `weapons_of_chaos.lua` loads 16 modules up front (`:142-196`), installs the registration owner, preview owner, shared-relic runtime and spirit runtime, then defines lifecycle callbacks (`:1007-1040`). The package lists every Lua file by name (29 of 30; `_woc_appearance_census.lua` is test-only data and is intentionally not listed), unlike cwv's wildcard.

**State ownership.** Modules receive an explicit context table with asserted members (`_woc_relic_registration_owner.lua:7-39`). No god-object. Registration is deferred to `StateInGameRunning.on_enter` and reports the gate that deferred it (`:47-60`, `:425-505`).

**Hook surface.** 46 hook call sites in source, 55 registrations at runtime (seven previewer hooks are installed once for `HeroPreviewer` and once for `MenuWorldPreviewer`, `_woc_mod_unit_preview.lua:390-442`). 16 call sites are previewer hooks, 13 are Chaos Wastes / backend hooks in the registration owner. `mod.update` runs four steppers every frame (`weapons_of_chaos.lua:1007-1012`).

**RPC channels (3).** `woc_relic_lease_intent_v1`, `woc_relic_lease_state_v1`, `woc_relic_lease_query_v1` (`_woc_shared_relic_runtime.lua:789-834`): a host-authoritative protocol that lets only one player in a lobby hold the Blightreaper, with host-migration handling.

**Vanilla tables mutated.** `ItemMasterList[woc_blightreaper]`, `NetworkLookup.item_names` (through the canonical helper, result checked, `_woc_relic_registration_owner.lua:486-494`), `NetworkLookup.rarities` + `RaritySettings`/`UISettings`/`Colors` ("Cursed" rarity, `_woc_cursed_rarity.lua`), `Weapons` (one private template), `WeaponTraits`/`WeaponProperties`/`BuffTemplates` rows, `NetworkLookup.inventory_packages` aliases.

**Persistence.** None of its own (0 `mod:set`). It can DELETE items from Crafting in Modded's store through `cim._cim277_delete_owned_ids` (`_woc_relic_registration_owner.lua:380-398`).

**Cross-mod dependencies.** `MoreItemsLibrary` (checked and reported, `:449-453`), `cim_dev`/`cim` private functions, shared copied libs.

**Assessment.** WOC is the same idea as cwv written later with the lessons applied: explicit dependencies, checked registration results, a real disable path (`weapons_of_chaos.lua:1028-1034`). It is coherent. Its problem is scope, not structure: one shipped item carries a distributed lease protocol (about 1,570 lines in `_woc_shared_relic*.lua`), a per-frame spirit system, a custom rarity and an attack-order editor, and almost none of that can be verified without two clients. It also copies cwv patterns that are themselves debt (the `_play_3p_anim` remap is now implemented three times: wt, cwv, WOC).

**Impact.** Whether the second application is harmless depends on each handler being idempotent, which I did not establish [unverified]. Preview transform and scale bugs are a standing cluster (#237, #692, #712, #747). Confirmation: with debug logging on, one inventory preview spawn prints both `[cwv preview hook] HeroPreviewer._spawn_item fired` and `MenuWorldPreviewer._spawn_item fired`.

**Recommendation.** Hook only the parent class for methods the child delegates, or set a re-entry flag on `self` for the duration of the outer call.

### A2-F16 | S2 | coupling | Both mods reach into Crafting in Modded's private functions and saved data

**Evidence.** cwv rewrites `item_key` and `skin` inside CIM's persisted `forged_weapons` table and saves it (`character_weapon_variants.lua:1215-1294`; persistence via `cim._cim_persist_crafts` or `cim:set`, `:1262-1265`), with an in-memory rollback but no backup copy. WOC deletes CIM-owned backend ids through `cim._cim277_delete_owned_ids` (`_woc_relic_registration_owner.lua:380-398`). Eight call sites use `cim._cim_get_craft`.

**Impact.** A change to CIM's storage shape can corrupt or lose a player's crafted weapons from a different mod's code path. The migration is attempted on every gameplay entry until it reports nothing to do.

**Recommendation.** CIM should expose two public functions (`migrate_item_keys(patches)` returning a receipt, and `delete_owned(ids)`), own the backup, and the two callers should use only those. Add an offline contract test in CIM for both.

### A2-F17 | S2 | architecture | WOC carries a host-authoritative "one holder per lobby" lease protocol for a single item

**Evidence.** `_woc_shared_relic.lua` (300 lines, state machine with host migration, reservations, rollback retry) and `_woc_shared_relic_runtime.lua` (1,274 lines, three RPC channels, hooks on `BackendUtils.set_loadout_item`, `HeroViewStateOverview._set_loadout_item`, `SimpleInventoryExtension.create_equipment_in_slot`, `KeepDecorationTrophyExtension._load_trophy`). That is 16% of the mod. The only in-game evidence is one check, `issue934_shared_relic_lease_contract`.

**Impact.** This can only be verified with two or more real clients (class C) including a host-migration case, in a project whose constraint is that nobody is testing. It hooks the equip path of every item for every player to serve one relic.

**Recommendation.** Owner decision (question 4). If lobby-uniqueness is not essential, remove the lease and keep the item; that deletes about 1,570 lines and three channels.

### A2-F18 | S2 | crash-safety | WOC's package lists Lua files one by one; a missing entry has already crashed startup twice

**Evidence.** `weapons_of_chaos/resource_packages/weapons_of_chaos/weapons_of_chaos.package` names 29 Lua files. `weapons_of_chaos/POSTMORTEMS.md` records #595: a new module not added to the list crashed the game at keep spawn in v0.1.11, and the same class regressed in v0.1.58 on 2026-08-31. cwv uses a wildcard (`"scripts/mods/character_weapon_variants/*"`). **Impact.** The gate treats the symptom; the cause is a list that must be kept in sync by hand.

**Recommendation.** Replace the Lua list with `"scripts/mods/weapons_of_chaos/*"` and keep the gate as a backstop.

### A2-F19 | S2 | network-coop | Chaos Wastes identity is gated by presence-only parity, and nobody has enumerated what the cwv keys ride on there

**Evidence.** `_om.install_deus_identities` maps each cwv item to a dedicated `deus_cwv_*` entry when `mod._cwv_peer_parity:applied_state() == "enabled"` (`_cwv_item_registration_owner.lua:587-600`, `_cwv_deus_identity.lua:58-64`). ### A2-F20 | S2 | architecture | Hard dependencies are not declared or checked by cwv

### A2-F21 | S3 | dead-code | Stubs, retired rows and test data ship in the bundle

### A2-F22 | S3 | maintainability | Defensive style hides intent

**Evidence.** 640 `pcall(` and 1,691 `rawget(` in 47k lines. Logging is `pcall(printf, ...)` because `mod:warning` posts to chat (`character_weapon_variants.lua:191-192`). ### A2-F24 | S2 | security | cwv removes the DLC requirement from clones of paid-DLC weapons, with an unverified note in the code

**Evidence.** `entry.required_dlc = nil` is unconditional (`_cwv_item_registration_owner.lua:499`); the comment above it says "POTENTIAL BUG: this is verified for the Empire greatsword units ... but NOT the Bretonnian units" (`:494-498`). 11 of the 25 base weapons carry `required_dlc` in vanilla (`lake`, `woods`, `grass`, `bless`, `holly`; section 2.3 row 3). ## 6. Testability and seams

### 6a. What the existing tests assert

**In-game checks (`_rt_register`).** cwv registers 112 checks (111 distinct names; `issue428_old_musket_retained_sweep` is registered from two files: `_cwv_old_musket_melee_owner.lua:263` and `_cwv_variant_bootstrap_owner.lua:66`). WOC registers 20. They run only from the chat commands.

A heuristic classification of 102 parsed cwv check bodies (`scripts\A2_rtchecks.py`; I read 4 of them in full, so treat the split as approximate):

| kind | count | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| flag / marker | 8 | compares a constant or an "installed" flag set by the installer itself; cannot fail when the behaviour regresses |
| table shape only | 17 | reads `ItemMasterList` / `WeaponSkins` / `Weapons` rows and compares fields; real, but equally runnable offline against fixtures |
| drives mod functions | 29 | calls cwv functions with synthetic inputs and checks the result (the useful kind) |
| undetermined | 48 | mixed; not individually read |

The decisive measurement: across the five in-game regression modules (about 5,500 lines) there are 0 reads of `Unit.local_scale` / `local_position` / `local_rotation`, 0 `World.*` calls, 1 `ScriptUnit.extension` reference, 3 `Unit.alive`, 7 `local_player`. So the suite almost never looks at the live game: no check reads the unit actually spawned in the player's hand, its transform, its package, its animation events or the HUD. It is an offline-style contract suite that happens to execute inside the game, where it gains access to the real vanilla tables and nothing else. The thing only an in-game check can do (class R: read engine state after a real equip) is the thing it does not do.

WOC's 19 parsed checks: 5 drive code, 5 are table shape, 9 undetermined; 2 always pass in retail because they need `io` (A2-F08).

**Offline tests (`qa/lua/tests`).** 140 files, 47,826 lines mention the two mods; 60 `loadfile` a real mod module and call it; 107 read mod source as text; about 37% of assertions are source-text pins (A2-F09). The executable ones are concentrated on the engine-free policy modules (wire policies, lifecycle, acquisition, combat-style policy, crowbill mode, WOC lease state machine, attack order). That part is genuinely good and should be kept.

**Static gates touching this family.** `qa/check_custom_unit_bundle_reachability.ps1` (custom units present in a root bundle), `qa/check_cwv_old_musket_asset_contract.ps1` and `..._compiled_contract.ps1` (Old Musket source and compiled asset pins), `qa/check_dofile_package_coverage.ps1`, `qa/check_appearance_contracts.ps1`. These are real machine checks. Nothing validates the variant catalog against the decompiled source.

**Asset pipeline (what is reproducible).** Greataxe (5 models) and Crowbill (6 models): scripted from downloaded archives by `tools/convert_greataxe_assets.ps1` / `convert_crowbill_assets.ps1` + Blender 4.4 + ImageMagick; unit, material and `.texture` files are generated from templates in the script; archive SHA-256 values are recorded (`tools/GREATAXE_ASSET_PIPELINE.md`). Old Musket: `convert_old_musket_assets.ps1` + `prepare_old_musket_fbx.py` with a numeric contract (`tools/old_musket_asset_contract.json`) that a QA gate checks. Blightreaper: `weapons_of_chaos/tools/export_blightreaper_fbx.py` from an unpacked game bundle. Limits: the inputs live outside the repo in untracked folders on one machine (`<repos>/_cwv_greataxe_sources`, `_cwv_crowbill_sources`, `%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\old-musket\...`), tool paths are hard-coded, and the three `bundleV2/*.mod_bundle` files are committed binaries. So: reproducible by the owner on PC-A, not by CI or another agent. Icons (`art_source/cwv_weapon_icons`, 9 PNG) and the Skarrik units have no scripted source that I found. Hand-made and unverifiable by script: every per-variant scale / offset / rotation value.

## 8. Questions for the owner

1. **Mixed lobbies.** Must a player WITHOUT cwv / WOC be able to share a lobby with a player who has them, with no crash and silent visual fallback (today's design goal, and the source of roughly a fifth of the code)? Or may the mods simply switch all their items off (swap to the base weapon and say so in chat) whenever any peer lacks the exact same build? The second answer removes most of the identity transport, husk path and exact-catalog machinery.
2. **Paid DLC.** cwv clears `required_dlc` on clones of weapons from five paid DLCs (`_cwv_item_registration_owner.lua:499`). Is giving those movesets to non-owners acceptable for the public Workshop release, or should the clone keep the requirement?
3. **Boundary with weapon_tweaker.** cwv silently unlocks four vanilla weapons across characters and patches six vanilla templates (A2-F11). Should those move to weapon_tweaker (which owns availability), stay in cwv behind a setting, or be removed?
4. **Blightreaper uniqueness.** Is "only one player in the lobby can hold the Blightreaper" essential? It costs about 1,570 lines and three network channels and can only be verified with two clients and a host migration.
5. **Combat Styles.** This is a moveset switcher for mostly vanilla weapons, 6,600 lines, 14% of cwv, with four open issues. Keep it in cwv, split it into its own mod, or freeze it at the families that already work?
6. **Old Musket scope.** The stance toggle, melee-slot musket, shared ammo pool and custom-mesh FX proxy make this one weapon the most expensive in the project (5,600 lines, four global engine hooks, six open issues). Is the full feature required, or is "a ranged musket with a custom model" enough?
7. **Unfinished content in the shipped build.** Dawi Mace family (placeholder meshes, #602 blocked on an art licence), Skarrik boss weapons (assets bundled, no item), smoke bomb probe (#343): ship as is, hide behind a dev flag, or remove until decided?
8. **Old saves.** Can the one-time bridges be deleted (the retired Imperial Longsword rows, the CIM style migration at `character_weapon_variants.lua:1215-1294`, the Infantry Spear purge)? That depends on whether any tester still has pre-July crafted items worth preserving.
