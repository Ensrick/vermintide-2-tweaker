# weapon_tweaker — Cross-Character Availability Coverage Audit

> READ-ONLY audit (generated by an analysis pass, do not hand-edit as a design
> doc). Measures, per receiver character, which cross-character weapons are
> carried into that receiver's Weapon Availability options vs. which are gaps,
> analogous skips, or CWV-managed.
>
> **Inputs used**
> - `scripts/mods/weapon_tweaker/wt_unlock_data.lua` — `weapon_unlock_map`
>   (actual availability) + `cwv_managed`.
> - `CROSS_CHARACTER_PORT_DECISIONS.md` — the decisions ledger (ADD tables,
>   analogous/identical SKIP tables).
> - **Ground-truth roster (independent):** decompiled `ItemMasterList` under
>   `C:/Users/danjo/source/repos/Vermintide-2-Source-Code/scripts/settings/`
>   (`item_master_list_exported.lua`, `_anvil`, `_paperweight`, `_morris`,
>   plus DLC lists `cog`/`lake`/`bless`/`shovel`/`woods` and the Carousel
>   `_morris/deus_weapons`). Base weapons = entries with `slot_type =
>   "melee"/"ranged"` and a `template` field; skins/illusions
>   (`slot_type = "weapon_skin"`), `_magic_*`, `vs_*`, `_preview`, `_tutorial`,
>   and career-skill weapons excluded.

## Headline findings

- **Roster = 83 distinct base weapons** (52 melee, 31 ranged) across the 5 characters.
- **Zero never-considered weapons.** The `weapon_unlock_map`'s distinct
  weapon-key universe is **exactly** those 83 keys. Every wieldable base weapon
  in the game appears somewhere in wt's availability system.
- The three **walked** receivers are essentially complete:
  - **Kruber** — 0 true gaps (every missing item is analogous or CWV).
  - **Kerillian** — 0 true gaps (every missing item is analogous).
  - **Saltzpyre non-WP** — 4 gaps, all *decided-but-not-baked* ports.
- The three **unwalked** receivers have no receiver section in the decisions
  doc (the `## Receiver: Bardin / Kerillian(as-owner) / Sienna` and the
  Warrior Priest walk-throughs are empty stubs), so their cross-character pools
  were never designed:
  - **Bardin** — only 5 of 65 cross-char weapons present.
  - **Warrior Priest** — only 1 of 40 cross-char *melee* weapons present (ranged forbidden by design).
  - **Sienna** — 0 of 69 cross-char weapons present.

## Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AVAILABLE** | Present in the `weapon_unlock_map` for that receiver. Unless noted "(some)", it is on ALL of the receiver's careers. |
| **GAP** | Missing from the receiver, NOT analogous, NOT CWV-managed, NOT the receiver's own weapon. The actionable output. |
| **ANALOGOUS(→native)** | Intentionally skipped per the decisions doc because it duplicates the named native/already-present weapon. |
| **CWV** | Listed in `cwv_managed`; wt deliberately does not add it (CWV owns the port). |
| **N/A (own)** | Weapon whose owning-character prefix equals the receiver — not a cross-character port; excluded from the receiver's cross-char tables. |
| **N/A (WP ranged)** | Warrior Priest only: bows/crossbows/firearms/staves are forbidden by project rule, so a missing ranged weapon is not a gap. |

Owning-character prefix scheme: `es_` = Kruber, `dr_` = Bardin, `wh_` = Saltzpyre, `we_` = Kerillian, `bw_` = Sienna.

## Method / caveats

**Roster source.** The 83-weapon ground-truth roster was extracted independently
from the decompiled `ItemMasterList` (files listed at top), by scanning for
entries with `slot_type = "melee"/"ranged"` and a `template` field, then
excluding illusions/skins (`slot_type = "weapon_skin"`), `_magic_*` rarity
duplicates, `vs_*` Versus variants, `_preview`/`_tutorial` items, and
career-skill weapons (`*_career_skill_weapon`, e.g. the Engineer Crank Gun,
Bounty Hunter Locus). Cross-checked against the repo's own `ITEM_LIST.md`
(item_type groupings) — consistent. **Confidence: high** that these 83 are the
complete set of wieldable player base weapons.

**Reconciliation result.** The `weapon_unlock_map`'s distinct weapon-key set is
exactly these 83 keys (no key in the unlock_map is absent from the roster, and
no roster weapon is absent from the unlock_map). Hence **zero never-considered
weapons.** (The 5 `*_deus_01` Carousel/"grass"-DLC weapons — Moonfire Bow,
Griffon-foot, Coruscation Staff, Trollhammer Torpedo, Kruber Spear & Shield —
are defined as indented table entries in `item_master_list_morris.lua`, not
`ItemMasterList.<key> =` lines, so an initial parse missed them; they were
added back after locating the base definitions. They are all present in wt.)

**Availability granularity.** Within every walked receiver, each AVAILABLE
cross-char weapon is present on ALL of that receiver's careers — the parser
found no "present on some careers but not others" cases. (Warrior Priest is a
single-career receiver by construction.)

**Ownership ambiguity to note:**
- `es_1h_flail` (Empire Flail) carries an `es_` prefix (→ Kruber by scheme) but
  its vanilla `can_wield` is `wh_zealot`/`wh_captain`/`wh_bountyhunter` — it is
  **Saltzpyre's** native weapon, not Kruber's. The decisions doc's Kruber
  section calls it "Kruber-native," which is inaccurate. This audit classifies
  it by prefix (Kruber-owned) but it should be treated as a Saltzpyre weapon;
  on Saltzpyre (WP and non-WP) it is native/AVAILABLE, not a cross-char port.
  All other 82 weapons' `can_wield` matches their key prefix's character.

**Analogous/CWV determination** relied on the prose SKIP tables in
`CROSS_CHARACTER_PORT_DECISIONS.md` and the `cwv_managed` table. For the three
unwalked receivers (Bardin, WP, Sienna) there are NO recorded analogous
decisions, so every missing non-own weapon is a formal gap; the `[~native]`
annotations in those sections are this audit's own likely-analogue assessments,
NOT recorded decisions.

**`wh_1h_falchion` overlap (flagged in the Kruber section):** it appears in both
`weapon_unlock_map` (Kruber careers, → AVAILABLE) and `cwv_managed` (Kruber
careers, → should-skip). These are contradictory; wt and CWV may both add it on
Kruber. Not counted as a gap, but worth resolving.
