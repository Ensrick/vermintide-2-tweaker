# Chaos Wastes Weapon Traits — Handbook Reference

Source: [Chaos Waste Mechanic Handbook (Steam Guide ID 2458224546)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2458224546), updated to Patch 6.10. Trait sections extracted verbatim 2026-05-15.

The handbook organizes CW-exclusive traits into two groups based on when they were added:

---

## Exclusive Weapon Trait — Original (6 traits)

These were the original CW-exclusive traits before Be'lakor's update.

### Regrowth
- **Description:** Critical hits heal 5 health. Restore Temporary HP. Can trigger multiple times per swing depends on the number of enemies you cleave (damage and stagger).
- **Available on:** Normal Melee Weapons, Shield Melee Weapons, Heavy Melee Weapons

### Shard Strike
- **Description:** Killing an armoured enemy sends metal shards to damage other nearby enemies. Creating an aura constantly damaging and causing mini stagger near you. The aura lasts 16 sec.
- **Available on:** Normal Melee Weapons, Shield Melee Weapons, Heavy Melee Weapons

### Bloodthirst
- **Description:** Increase Attack Speed by 2% for every 5 kills (Up to 5 times). Resets if you do not kill anything for 30 sec. Also reset if you switch weapon.
- **Available on:** Normal Melee Weapons, Shield Melee Weapons, Heavy Melee Weapons

### Deadeye
- **Description:** Each headshot increases the damage of the next attack by 10%. (up to 20 times). If you hit an enemy anywhere other than the head, 1 stack gets removed. Can be triggered on normal enemy head and stormfiend control panel. Also reset if you switch weapon.
- **Available on:** Normal Melee Weapons, Shield Melee Weapons, Heavy Melee Weapons, All Range Weapons Except Moonfire Bow

### Divine Shield
- **Description:** Damage taken is reduced to 20 damage or half of its original value, whichever is highest.
- **Available on:** Shield Melee Weapons

### Shockwave
- **Description:** Critical hits stagger nearby enemies.
- **Available on:** Heavy Melee Weapons, All Range Weapons Except Moonfire Bow

Added in the Be'lakor DLC update.

### Asuryan's Wrath
- **Description:** Melee Attacks that kill enemies have a 50% chance to deal damage to an additional nearby enemy. Spread the killing blow melee hit damage.
- **Available on:** All melee weapons (all 5 careers)

- **Description:** Critical strikes trigger a chain lightning that jumps to the nearest 5 enemies. Deal damage equal to the attack that triggered it. Ignore armour.
- **Available on:** Sword, Mace, Axe, Hammer, Axe, Sword, Falchion, Hammer, Dagger, Sword, Flaming Flail, Mace

### Taal's Twinned Arrow
- **Description:** Ranged attacks now fire one additional projectile.
- **Available on:** Handgun, Repeater Handgun, Longbow, Handgun, Masterwork Pistol, Crossbow, Throwing Axe, Trollhammer, Drakefire Pistol, Hagbane, Swiftbow, Longbow, Volley Crossbow, Javelin, Moonfire Bow, Brace of Pistol, Repeater Pistol, Crossbow, Volley Crossbow

### Vaul's Anvil
- **Description:** While wielding your melee weapon all attacks made against you count as being blocked. When your block is broken you lose this effect for 10 seconds.
- **Available on:** Sword Shield, Mace Shield, Breton Sword Shield, Spear Shield, Axe Shield, Hammer Shield, Spear Shield, Hammer Shield, Flail Shield

### Addaioth's Splendour
- **Description:** Every 30 seconds, ranged Critical Hits explode for 10% of their Damage in area around the target. Has cooldown of 10 seconds instead of 30. Deal damage related to Hero Power, instead of original hit damage. Stagger enemies.
- **Available on:** Handgun, Repeater Handgun, Longbow, Handgun, Masterwork Pistol, Crossbow, Throwing Axe, Drakefire Pistol, Hagbane, Swiftbow, Longbow, Volley Crossbow, Javelin, Moonfire Bow, Deepwood Staff, Brace of Pistol, Repeater Pistol, Crossbow, Volley Crossbow, Fireball Staff, Bolt Staff

- Follow Up, Anatha Raema's Talons, Rhya's Thorns, Asaph's Endless Quiver, Vaul's Tempo, Quetzl's Repulsion, Huanchi's Fangs, Anath Raema's Swiftness, Myrmidia's Great Leveller, Asuryan's Wrath, Manann's Tempest, Taal's Twinned Arrow, Vaul's Anvil, Addaioth's Splendour (Be'lakor update)
